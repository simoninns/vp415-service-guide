# Module F - Motor + sequence

Disc motor drive with the start-up and braking sequence.

## Overview

The motor + sequence module drives the **turntable motor** — a brushless motor
with Hall elements for position sensing. The board carries five distinct
groups:

- **IC7202, the MDS-IC** — handles communication between the motor and the
  rest of the player and supplies the drive voltages for the output amplifiers.
- **The Hall element comparators** — pass rotor position to the MDS-IC
  continuously.
- **The sequence logic** around transistors 7020–7022 — start, brake, motor
  control and current limiting.
- **IC7230, the pulse-width modulator** — turns the drive voltage into a
  duty-cycle-controlled input for the MDS-IC.
- **The output stages** — supply the coil currents, derived from the
  commutating voltages the MDS-IC produces.

## Circuit description

In the start condition `TTM` is high and reaches the MDS-IC through buffers
7001 and 7002. While the motor is turning below 1500 rpm, the MDS-IC's tacho
output `TSP` is low, so the sequence circuit blocks 7021, its collector goes
high and switch 7201-4C closes. The high from `TTM` then reaches pulse-width
modulator 7230-2A, giving a low duty cycle and accelerating the motor; diodes
6001 and 6002 limit the charging current of 2031.

7230-2A compares that control voltage with a sawtooth of about 17.6 kHz,
generated by transistor 7023 and capacitor 2030 from the clock in IC7202.
**R3029 sets the amplitude of that sawtooth, and it is the module's first
adjustment.**

The full text — start, brake, run and current-limit conditions — is in
[chapter 7, module F](../../circuit-description/modules.md#module-f).

## Adjustments

Two adjustments, both around pulse-width modulator IC7230.

!!! info "Required"

    Test disc · scope · voltmeter

    Rotating disc.

**1) R3029 — sawtooth signal**

- Measure the sawtooth signal on pin 3 of IC7230-2A (Fig. F1 on the sheet).
- Adjust R3029 for a peak amplitude of **5.2 V ± 0.1 V**.

**2) R3049 — current limiter**

- Measure the DC voltage on pin 7 of IC7230.
- Adjust R3049 until it is **3.2 V ± 0.1 V**.

**Adjustment when an item is replaced**

| Replaced | Adjust |
| --- | --- |
| IC7230 | R3029, R3049 |

## Modification levels

The module shipped at level 5 and went to level 6, which gathers four separate
changes:

- C2010–C2015 deleted and C2901–C2903 (1.2 nF) added, to cure **white dots on
  screen**.
- C2020 4 μF → 1 μF, improving lock-in on 6″ and 8″ discs — this is fault
  symptom **A 3**, the start-up problem when loading a 6″ or 8″ disc.
- C2002 changed from a chip to a polyester 330 nF and R3015 to a metal-film
  1k5, because the tolerance and stability of the motor control loop were out
  of specification.
- IC7260 MC1458P1 → MC34002BP, improving GOTO on CLV discs.
