# Playing a disc

Section 2 of the operating instructions, page 11. The whole of ordinary
operation, in one page: switch on, put a disc in, and it plays.

## Switching on

Switch on the player using the mains `ON/OFF` switch at the rear. Check that
the `REPLAY` switch is in the `OFF` position. **The `STANDBY` indicator will
light.**

Press the `ON/STANDBY` button; the `STANDBY` indicator will then flash. A
warning beep is given to indicate that no disc has yet been loaded.

!!! note "The fan is meant to speed up"

    The VP415 contains a fan to maintain the correct operating temperature. If
    a certain temperature is exceeded, the speed of this fan will automatically
    increase; **this is quite normal and not a fault indication.**

## Inserting a disc

Press the `EJECT` button; the disc-tray will partly open. Pull the tray out all
the way. Remove the disc from its packaging and place it on the tray with the
desired label uppermost.

## Automatic play

Gently push the disc-tray forward; it will be drawn inside automatically. The
disc will build up to speed — **1500 r.p.m., in approximately 10 seconds** —
and the `STANDBY` indicator flashes during the start-up period.

As soon as the correct speed has been reached, the `STANDBY` indicator goes out
and either the `CAV` or the `CLV` indicator lights, according to the type of
disc loaded. Play then commences.

When the end of the disc is reached, the player returns rapidly to the first
track, gives a warning beep, and enters the pause mode. **If no further command
is given within 2 minutes, the player goes to standby.**

If repeated playing of the disc is required, the `REPLAY` switch should be in
the `ON` position, which is confirmed by the `REPLAY` indicator. In this case
**the controls on the front of the player are disabled**.

## To stop play and remove the disc

To terminate play at any time, press the `EJECT` button on the front of the
player. When the disc comes to a standstill, the player ejects the disc and
goes to the standby condition.

!!! info "What the player is doing during those ten seconds"

    The start-up sequence between pressing the tray home and the picture
    appearing is the same sequence the diagnostic software checks step by step:
    tray in, disc reflection, slide position, tilt, laser on, focus, motor
    speed, frame lock. If it stops part-way, that is where an
    [error code](../repair/error-codes.md) comes from, and
    [fault-finding chart 2](../repair/fault-finding.md) walks the same ground.
