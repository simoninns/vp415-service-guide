# System

Chapter 3 is the whole player at once: the lay-out, the vocabulary, and the
three block diagrams. Read it before you go looking at any single module —
almost every fault-finding step in chapter 6 assumes you have.

| Page | Manual | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Module and connector lay-out](module-layout.md) | 019 | Where every module and connector physically is, with an annotated photograph beside the drawing |
| [Alphabetical signal listing](signal-listing.md) | 020 | All 243 signal mnemonics, their meanings and active levels |
| [Wiring diagrams](wiring-diagrams.md) | 021–023 | Every interconnection: the player (WDP) and the sandwich (WDS) |
| [Block diagrams](block-diagrams.md) | 024–026 | Control routes, audio/video signal path, and the servo loops |

The manual also binds *connections of semiconductors* at the end of this
chapter, but files it under chapter 2 on its own contents page — so this site
puts it there, as
[connections of semiconductors](../general-service/semiconductor-connections.md).
