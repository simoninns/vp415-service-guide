# Wiring diagrams

Two sheets, both fold-outs, showing every wire and every connector pin between
modules. The player diagram covers the VP410 core; the sandwich diagram covers
the boards that make it a VP415.

Use them with the [module and connector lay-out](module-layout.md), which shows
where each of these connectors physically is, and with the
[signal listing](signal-listing.md) for what the names on the wires mean.

## Wiring diagram — player (WDP)

The widest sheet in the manual: three A4 panels, spanning service manual pages
021 and 022. Click it to open the full 300 dpi scan, which is legible down to
individual pin numbers.

## Wiring diagram — sandwich (WDS)

The sandwich is the second card cage fitted only to the VP415: the CPU and data
grabber, the LV-ROM decoder and the video mixer, and the SCSI interface out to
the rear panel.
