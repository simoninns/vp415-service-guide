# List of electrical parts

This is the **collective** list: the standard components used across the whole
player, in one place. Components specific to one module are on that module's
own page, in its electrical parts list.

!!! note "Unprogrammed EPROMs"

    The two types marked `*` in the manual — `TMS27128-20JL` (4822 209 71312)

    programmed part numbers, and dumps of their contents, are on the
    [firmware](../reference/firmware.md) page.
