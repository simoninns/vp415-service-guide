# Exploded view drawings

Five drawings, each numbered to the parts lists. Find the part on the drawing,
read its item number, then look the number up in
[mechanical parts](mechanical-parts.md).

Four of the five also exist as cleaned-up redraws from the community
disassembly guide — same drawing, background and page furniture removed. Those
are shown first because they are easier to read on screen; the manual sheet
follows each one.

assembly, the mains cord, the SCART cable and the text plate.

## Optical deck

Items 101–157: the slide drive assembly, the turntable motor assembly, the
toothed belt, and the ATC motor, belt and drive assembly.

!!! warning "Where the deck is repairable"

    The slide drive and the Automatic Tilt Control assemblies are the two parts
    of the deck the manual allows to be repaired. The Deck Electronics and the
    Laser Detection Unit are not — see
    [remarks](../general-service/remarks.md), section 5.
