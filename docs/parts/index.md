# Parts

Chapter 5 is the ordering chapter: five exploded drawings and three lists of
Philips service code numbers. Find the part on a drawing, read its item number,
look the number up in the list.

| Page | Manual | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Exploded views](exploded-views.md) | 102–105, 119 | Cabinet, sandwich, front loader, disc drive and optical deck, with item numbers |
| [Mechanical parts](mechanical-parts.md) | 120–121 | Fixing materials, optical deck, front loader, disc drive, sandwich, cabinet, sockets and connectors |
| [Electrical parts](electrical-parts.md) | 122–124 | The collective list: ICs, transistors, diodes and standard resistors |

Components specific to one module are not here — they are on that module's own
page, in its electrical parts list. See [modules A to Z](../modules/index.md).

!!! note "Where pages 119–124 sit in the binder"

    The manual binds the optical deck exploded view and all three parts lists
    *after* the chapter 7 divider, on service manual pages 119 to 124. They
    belong to chapter 5, and are placed in chapter 5 here.
