# Overview

Chapter 1 of the service manual is three sheets of reference data — the numbers
you check before you plug the player in, and the panel and pin identifications
you need before you probe anything.

| Page | Manual | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Technical data](technical-data.md) | 004 | Disc, laser, mains, video, audio, genlock and LV-ROM specifications |
| [Controls, indicators and connections](controls-and-connections.md) | 005 | The front and rear panels, with the manual's numbering for all 18 rear connections |
| [Connector pinning](connector-pinning.md) | 006 | Pin assignments for the Euroconnector, the RGB DIN input, RS232-C and SCSI |

The rear-panel numbers on the controls page are used throughout the site: when
a fault-finding chart or a module page says "at connector 12", it means the
CVBS OUT socket.
