# Warnings

Service manual page 009 carries four warnings. They are reproduced here in
full, and they are the first thing to read before the cabinet comes off.

!!! danger "1. Laser radiation"

    The Laser Detection Unit (LDU) in the optical deck has been equipped with a
    semiconductor laser. This laser emits **invisible** light which is focussed
    on the disc by the objective.

    If the objective would be removed in case of repair, the laser light exits
    from the objective aperture. **Avoid looking directly into the laser beam,
    as this might cause permanent injury to the eye.**

!!! warning "2. Replacement of modules"

    Before replacing a module upon repair, first the mains switch should be
    switched off. This should be done to prevent damage to the circuits on the
    modules.

!!! danger "3. Service position of the set"

    If measurements or repair require that the set is placed on its side (90°
    position), this may only be done when a **6" test disc** is played on the
    optical deck and the front loader has been removed. If a disc with a larger
    diameter is used (8" or 12"), the risk that the disc will come loose from
    the turntable (motor) and cause injury to people in the vicinity will be
    too great. Also ensure that the disc is always locked on the turntable by
    the magnetic disc clamping piece — see [service tools](service-tools.md).

    In the above-mentioned 90° position of the set not all signals will be
    present according to specification. **Adjustments and checks for correct
    functioning are therefore only allowed in the horizontal position of the
    set.**

!!! warning "4. The 6-inch test disc"

    The 6" test disc may only be played when the front loader has been removed.
    With mounted front loader playback of 8" and 12" discs is possible.

## What the manual does not say

Two hazards the 1986 manual takes for granted, and a repairer today should not:

- **The supply module runs off the mains.** [Module T](../modules/t-supply/index.md)
  is a switched-mode supply whose primary side and reservoir capacitors stay
  live and charged after the player is unplugged. Discharge before probing.
- **ESD kills silently.** The photodiodes and the laser in the optical deck are
  more sensitive than MOS ICs — see [service hints](service-hints.md).
