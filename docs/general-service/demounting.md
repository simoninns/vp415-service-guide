# Demounting

The manual's demounting instructions are one bifold sheet of line art with no
prose at all. This page reproduces the sheet and, beside it, the cleaned-up
line art from the community disassembly guide — the same drawings, redrawn
without the page furniture, one step at a time.

!!! danger "Before you start"

    Unplug the player and read the [warnings](warnings.md). The supply module
    stays charged after the mains lead comes out, and the deck's laser is
    invisible.

## Order of work

1. **Push the disc tray out.** With no power, the tray is released through the
   hole in the top cover with a small screwdriver.
2. **Remove the upper case and the front loader.**
3. **Remove the optical deck**, or work on it in place.
4. **Remove Analog I/O module U** — the rear panel it is fixed to comes with it.
5. **Remove the sandwich** — VP415 only.

Screw sizes and the speed-nut technique are on the manual sheet; the fixing
materials themselves are in the
[list of mechanical parts](../parts/mechanical-parts.md).

    The manual forbids component-level repair of the Deck Electronics and the
    Laser Detection Unit — see [remarks](remarks.md), section 5. The slide
    drive and ATC assemblies *are* repairable.
