# General service

Chapter 2 is everything that applies to the whole player rather than to one
module. Read the [warnings](warnings.md) first; the rest you will come back to
as you work.

| Page | Manual | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Remarks](remarks.md) | 008 | How the manual is organised, the item-number conversion between diagrams and PCB silkscreen, the hidden switches on module U |
| [Warnings](warnings.md) | 009 | Laser radiation, module replacement, the 90° service position, the 6" test disc |
| [Modification levels](modification-levels.md) | 010 | Where the level of the set, a module and the EPROM software is marked, and the `?=` revision request |
| [Adjustments](adjustments.md) | 011–012 | What must be adjusted after a module swap, the equipment needed, and the video adjustments survey |
| [Demounting](demounting.md) | 013 | Taking the player apart, step by step |
| [Service hints](service-hints.md) | 014 | ESD, and handling chip components |
| [Service tools](service-tools.md) | 015–016 | The Philips repair set with code numbers, and each tool in use |
| [List of used symbols](symbols.md) | 017 | The circuit-diagram legend, resistor markings and voltage codes |
| [Connections of semiconductors](semiconductor-connections.md) | 027 | Package pinouts and a type-to-package cross-reference |

!!! danger "Three things that will hurt you or the player"

    - The **laser** in the optical deck is invisible and the objective can emit
      it directly if the cabinet is open. See [warnings](warnings.md).
    - **Module T** is a mains-side switched-mode supply and stays charged after
      the lead comes out.
    - **ESD** destroys the deck photodiodes and the laser more readily than it
      destroys MOS ICs. See [service hints](service-hints.md).
