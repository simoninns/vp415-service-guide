# List of used symbols

The circuit diagrams use a Philips house legend, printed in four languages on
one bifold sheet. The sheet itself is the reference — the symbols are drawings
and there is no useful way to put them in a table — but the two coded parts of
it, the resistor markings and the voltage letters, are transcribed here so that
they are searchable.

## Resistor markings

The mark inside the left end of a resistor symbol gives its rating and
tolerance:

| Mark | Rating |
| --- | --- |
| Warning triangle above the body | Safety resistor |
| Solid triangle | 0.2 W, ≤220 kΩ − 5%; (CR16) >270 kΩ − 10% |
| Small filled square | 0.33 W, <1 MΩ − 5%; (SFR25) >1 MΩ − 10% |
| Filled circle | 0.5 W, ≤1 MΩ − 5%; (CR37) >1 MΩ − 10% |
| Small open square in a cut corner | 0.33 W, MR25 − 1% |
| Hatched corner | 0.5 W, ≤1 MΩ − 5%; (CR52) >1 MΩ − 10% |
| Solid filled corner | 1 W, ≤1.6 MΩ − 5%; (CR68) >1.6 MΩ − 10% |
| Plus sign in a box | 0.5 W high-voltage resistor (VR37) |

A warning triangle beside a capacitor marks a safety capacitor. The capacitor
body styles distinguish ceramic plate, metallised polyester flat film, and
miniature electrolytic types.

## Voltage code letters

A capacitor's working voltage is given as a single letter:

| Code | Voltage | Code | Voltage | Code | Voltage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a | 2.5 V | g | 40 V | r | 250 V |
| b | 4 V | h | 63 V | s | 350 V |
| c | 6.3 V | j | 100 V | u | 400 V |
| d | 10 V | l | 125 V | v | 500 V |
| e | 16 V | m | 150 V | w | 630 V |
| f | 25 V | q | 200 V | x | 1000 V |
| | | | | y | 1600 V |

## Functional blocks

The remaining three columns of the sheet are functional block symbols, in four
languages: signal-processing blocks (sawtooth pulse converter, PCM, pulse
duration modulation, sync separator, FM detector, phase discriminator,
detector, level detector, phase-changing network, rejection, bandpass and
low-pass filters, mixer stage), source and gain blocks (high-pass filter, HF
generator, sawtooth and square-wave generators, delay element, limiter,
positive- and negative-going step functions, emitter follower, automatically
controlled amplifier, mixer, amplifier, differential amplifier, amplifier with
open output, electronic switch), and logic (common control block, shift
register `SRG`, output `Q`, open-collector output, command input `G`, chip
enable `CE`, bidirectional `00`, inverter, OR, NOR, AND and NAND gates with
their truth tables, buffer, inverting buffer, buffer with open output).
